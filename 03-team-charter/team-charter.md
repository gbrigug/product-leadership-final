# Lead and Develop High-Performing Teams, Module 3 Lab

## Name the situation
- **Who they are (role, not name), what you have observed, and how long it has been happening.:** I have a senior product manager on my team that started within the last year. While she lacks domain knowledge, she has good product fundamentals, asks good questions, and communicates professionally. Due to the domain knowledge gap, she isn't always very present in calls. She feels shy to speak up, take charge, or propose next steps. The first 6-8 months can be attributed to a steep learning curve, as we're in a complex fintech space. However, the last 3 months have been more of a gap/opportunity area for her to be more direct & lead some initiatives.

## Make a diagnosis
- **Your diagnosis, plus one sentence on why. Is your frustration with their behavior, or with a decision you made?:** Feedback problem. I need to be better about distinctly calling out exact examples where she should speak up & encouraging her to do so in future meetings.

## Write your opening line
- **One next action I will take in the next two weeks is:** Approach her around a major Q4 project she's involved with & clearly communicate the gaps we need her help with driving.
- **The first sentence of the conversation I need to have is:** In Q4 planning, ambiguity and challenges from leadership came up around one of the initiatives you planned. I noticed you didn't defend your position there or take ownership to find those answers. This weakens our reputation & can negatively impact the group's perspective on our strategy and leadership. How can we approach questions in the future?

## AI role-play
- **After you step out: what did the role-play change about how you will open this conversation for real?:** My feedback was too direct. I needed to reframe my initial questioning to create a safer, more constructive environment.

## Refine and complete your charter
- **What We Own. What this team owns.:** The core audio/visual player, multi-session resolution tracks, and in-app efficacy tracking that guide users from acute stress through post-module progress.
- **What is out of scope.:** Onboarding intake, retention notifications/paywalls, AI model logic, and clinical/psychiatric care.
- **Cross-boundary decisions that need a joint call.:** 1) AI check-in integration.
2) Onboarding to Module handoff.
3) Transitioning Resolved Users.
- **How We Decide. Who decides feature and scope calls.:** PM's define & own strategy in their respective domains. Team can be consulted to provide input.
- **How cross-team conflicts escalate.:** Overlap situations require peer input & joint decision within 48 hours, else Product Leaders are brought in to help drive decisions.
- **Who resolves escalations from outside the team.:** Product leaders will give verdict in 24 hours.
