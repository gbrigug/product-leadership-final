Team Charter: Ownership & Alignment
Fable Growth & Core Product Strategy
1. Ownership Boundaries
What We Own
  • Core Player: Audio/visual session experience[cite: 3].
  • Resolution Tracks: Structured multi-session programs[cite: 3].
  • Efficacy Tracking: In-app progress & symptom metrics[cite: 3].
  • Acute to Post-Acute: Guiding user recovery steps[cite: 3].
What Is Out of Scope
  • Onboarding Intake: Account setup & pre-module flows[cite: 3].
  • Retention & Paywalls: Push alerts, emails & subscriptions[cite: 3].
  • AI Engine Logic: Prompt models & underlying conversational AI[cite: 3].
  • Clinical Care: Psychiatric treatment & clinical diagnosis[cite: 3].
Cross-Boundary Joint Calls
  • 1) AI Check-in Integration: Placement and triggers inside playback[cite: 3].
  • 2) Onboarding to Module Handoff: Routing intake into first track[cite: 3].
  • 3) Transitioning Resolved Users: Moving users to post-acute paths[cite: 3].
2. How We Decide
Domain Decision Principle: PMs define and own strategy within their respective domains[cite: 3]. Surrounding teams and cross-functional partners are consulted to provide input prior to execution[cite: 3].
3. Conflict Escalation Path
Level 1
Peer Alignment
Within 48 Hours
Overlap situations require peer input & joint decision making among domain PMs[cite: 3].
►
Level 2
Product Leader Escalation
Brought in at 48 hrs
If peer agreement is not reached, Product Leaders are brought in to help drive decisions[cite: 3].
►
Level 3
Leader Verdict
Within 24 Hours
Product Leaders evaluate trade-offs and issue a binding final decision within 24 hours[cite: 3].
