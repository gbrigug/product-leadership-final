NOW Active Strategic Bets • Outcome-based ("We bet [action] will [outcome] for [who]")
Bet 1: Frictionless Onboarding & Modular Activation KR1
We bet streamlining initial onboarding and removing friction to first-resolution modules will increase 48-hour modular activation for users seeking immediate digital therapy for non-critical mental health challenges.
Bet 2: Daily Habit Formation Mechanics KR2
We bet introducing daily habit-forming engagement mechanics will grow repeat behavior by 5% over 30 days for active users attempting to build a consistent self-help routine.
Bet 3: AI Contextual Re-engagement Prompts KR2
We bet delivering AI-personalized check-in prompts based on past interactions will drive higher 30-day user retention and re-engagement for recurring users looking for relevant ongoing support.
